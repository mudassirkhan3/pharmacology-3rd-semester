Frequently Asked Questions

What is included?

Full access includes syllabus-aligned chapter notes, high-yield tables, drug mnemonics, concept animations, interactive simulators, chapter MCQs, and past examination papers.

Who is this portal for?

It is built specifically for 3rd semester BSN (Bachelor of Science in Nursing) students preparing for their Pharmacology term and semester final examinations.

Is Chapter 1 available as a demo?

Yes! Chapter 1 (Basic Principles & ADME) is 100% free to explore without needing any login or access code.

How long is access?

Your one-time access remains active right up until your December final examinations.

How do I get my access code?

Click on "Get Access" or "Student Login", enter your access code, or follow the payment instructions provided in the access prompt.

Is this an official UHS website?

No. This is a student-developed educational resource designed to support BSN Pharmacology learning and examination preparation.
