Course Learning Outcomes & Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be equipped to master the following clinical and theoretical competencies:

01. Systemic Drug Effects

Discuss drug impacts on gastrointestinal, genitourinary, and immunological systems.

02. Indications & Disorders

Relate drug knowledge directly to indications for treating various systemic disorders.

03. Clinical Rationale

Explain the precise clinical rationale for selecting specific medications for patients.

04. Nursing Interventions

Select appropriate, safe nursing interventions for medications given in clinical settings.

05. Client & Family Education

Impart effective patient and family medication education based on individual needs.

06. Nursing Process Integration

Utilize the nursing process to evaluate the necessity and efficacy of prescribed drugs.

07. Legal & Ethical Issues

Integrate legal and ethical considerations into safe medication administration practices.

08. Research & New Drugs

Incorporate research findings as a foundational base for nursing practice and drug development.

09. Holistic Skill Application

Incorporate cognitive, interpersonal, and technical skills from behavioral and natural sciences.

10. Accurate Dosage Calculations

Calculate exact drug dosages accurately when administering oral and parenteral medications.
