Oncology Pharmacology Hub

⛓️

Alkylating Agents

DNA cross-linking causing strand breakage (e.g., Nitrogen mustards, Platinums).
🧩

Antimetabolites

Mimics natural bases to inhibit S-phase enzyme function (e.g., 5-FU, Methotrexate).
🧬

Antitumor Antibiotics

Intercalation into DNA and Topoisomerase interference (e.g., Doxorubicin).
🧶

Microtubule Inhibitors

Inhibiting mitotic spindle formation to halt cell division (e.g., Vinca alkaloids).
🔑

Hormones & Antagonists

Modulating receptor-mediated signaling pathways (e.g., Tamoxifen).
🛡️

Monoclonal Antibodies

Targeted binding to surface antigens for immune-mediated lysis.
📡

Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors

Blocking intracellular growth signaling pathways (e.g., Imatinib).

Nursing Safety Protocols

  • 1
    Bone Marrow Suppression: Daily ANC/Platelet monitoring; initiate neutropenic precautions at first sign of decline.
  • 2
    Vesicant Vigilance: Confirm patent blood return before infusion; keep antidote/hyaluronidase kits accessible.
  • 3
    Hypersensitivity Watch: Monitor for anaphylaxis during monoclonal antibody infusions; pre-medicate as prescribed.
