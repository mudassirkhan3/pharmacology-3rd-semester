Chemotherapy Classification

🔗

Alkylating Agents

DNA cross-linking causing strand breakage (e.g., Nitrogen mustards, Platinums).
🧪

Antimetabolites

S-phase specific enzyme inhibition preventing DNA synthesis (e.g., 5-FU, Methotrexate).
🎸

Antitumor Antibiotics

DNA intercalation and interference with topoisomerase (e.g., Doxorubicin, Bleomycin).
⚖️

Microtubule Inhibitors

Inhibiting mitotic spindle formation to halt cell division (e.g., Vinca alkaloids, Taxanes).
🧬

Steroid Hormones & Antagonists

Modulating receptor-mediated signaling pathways (e.g., Tamoxifen, Prednisone).
🎯

Monoclonal Antibodies

Targeted binding to specific cell-surface antigens for immune-mediated lysis.
⚡

Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors

Blocking signal transduction pathways vital for tumor growth (e.g., Imatinib).

Oncology Ward Protocols & Nursing Responsibilities

  • 1
    Bone Marrow Suppression Monitoring: Daily assessment of ANC (Absolute Neutrophil Count) and platelet levels. Implement neutropenic precautions immediately if counts drop.
  • 2
    Vesicant/Extravasation Management: Meticulous checks for blood return before and during administration of high-risk agents. Know your antidote protocols (e.g., Hyaluronidase).
  • 3
    Infusion Reaction Surveillance: Rigorous monitoring for hypersensitivity (anaphylaxis, fever, chills) during monoclonal antibody administration. Pre-medication as ordered.
  • 4
    Antiemetic Regimen Adherence: Proactive administration of 5-HT3 antagonists/corticosteroids to prevent chemotherapy-induced nausea/vomiting (CINV).
