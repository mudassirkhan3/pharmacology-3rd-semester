Alkylating Agents IMP FOR EXAM

DNA cross-linking causing strand breakage (e.g., Nitrogen mustards, Platinums).
🧩

Antimetabolites IMP FOR EXAM

Mimics natural bases to inhibit S-phase enzyme function (e.g., 5-FU, Methotrexate).
🧬

Antitumor Antibiotics IMP FOR EXAM

Intercalation into DNA and Topoisomerase interference (e.g., Doxorubicin).
🧶

Microtubule Inhibitors IMP FOR EXAM

Inhibiting mitotic spindle formation to halt cell division (e.g., Vinca alkaloids, Taxanes).
🔑

Hormones & Antagonists

Modulating receptor-mediated signaling pathways (e.g., Tamoxifen, Leuprolide).
🛡️

Monoclonal Antibodies IMP FOR EXAM

Targeted binding to surface antigens for immune-mediated lysis (e.g., Rituximab, Trastuzumab).
📡

Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors

Blocking intracellular growth signaling pathways (e.g., Imatinib, Erlotinib).

Nursing Safety Protocols

  • 1
    Bone Marrow Suppression: Daily ANC/Platelet monitoring; initiate neutropenic precautions at first sign of decline. IMP FOR EXAM
  • 2
    Vesicant Vigilance: Confirm patent blood return before infusion; keep antidote/hyaluronidase kits accessible. IMP FOR EXAM
  • 3
    Hypersensitivity Watch: Monitor for anaphylaxis during monoclonal antibody infusions; pre-medicate as prescribed.

📊 Master Oncology Pharmacology Summary Table

High-yield comparison across all 7 major antineoplastic & targeted oncology drug categories.

Class Prototype Drugs Mechanism of Action Cell Cycle Specificity Primary Clinical Uses Key Toxicity / Nursing Alerts
1. Alkylating Agents Cyclophosphamide, Cisplatin, Carboplatin Covalently cross-links DNA strands, causing breakage and replication arrest Cell-Cycle Non-Specific (CCNS) Lymphomas, leukemias, ovarian, lung, testicular cancers Hemorrhagic cystitis (Cyclophosphamide - give Mesna); Nephrotoxicity & Ototoxicity (Cisplatin)
2. Antimetabolites Methotrexate, 5-Fluorouracil (5-FU), Cytarabine Inhibits DNA/RNA synthesis by mimicking natural purines/pyrimidines S-Phase Specific ALL, breast cancer, colorectal cancer, head & neck tumors Severe bone marrow suppression, mucositis; Leucovorin rescue required with high-dose Methotrexate
3. Antitumor Antibiotics Doxorubicin, Bleomycin Intercalates into DNA strands & inhibits Topoisomerase II; generates free radicals Cell-Cycle Non-Specific (CCNS) Hodgkin lymphoma, breast, ovarian, sarcoma, thyroid cancers Cardiotoxicity / CHF (Doxorubicin - monitor LVEF & lifetime dose limit); Pulmonary fibrosis (Bleomycin)
4. Microtubule Inhibitors Vincristine, Vinblastine, Paclitaxel Binds tubulin to disrupt mitotic spindle formation and arrest mitosis M-Phase Specific Leukemias, Kaposi sarcoma, breast, lung, ovarian cancers Peripheral neuropathy & paralytic ileus (Vincristine - NEVER IV intrathecal); Hypersensitivity (Paclitaxel)
5. Hormones & Antagonists Tamoxifen, Anastrozole, Leuprolide Blocks or modulates hormone receptors (estrogen/androgen) driving tumor growth Cell-Cycle Non-Specific (CCNS) Hormone receptor-positive breast cancer, prostate cancer Thromboembolism & endometrial cancer risk (Tamoxifen); Hot flashes & osteoporosis
6. Monoclonal Antibodies Rituximab, Trastuzumab (Herceptin) Binds specific tumor surface antigens (CD20, HER2), marking cells for immune lysis Targeted Therapy Non-Hodgkin lymphoma (Rituximab), HER2+ breast cancer (Trastuzumab) Infusion-related anaphylaxis (pre-medicate with diphenhydramine/acetaminophen); Cardiotoxicity (Trastuzumab)
7. Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors (TKIs) Imatinib (Gleevec), Erlotinib Competitively blocks ATP binding site on intracellular BCR-ABL / EGFR kinases Targeted Therapy Chronic Myelogenous Leukemia (CML - Imatinib), Non-small cell lung cancer Fluid retention / periorbital edema, diarrhea, acneiform rash, hepatic enzyme elevations

🌳 Chapter 5 Oncology Classification Tree

Visual hierarchy based on cell-cycle specificity and targeted mechanism pathways.

🧬 1. Cell-Cycle Specific (CCS) Cytotoxic Agents
S-Phase (DNA Synthesis): Antimetabolites (Methotrexate, 5-FU, Cytarabine) M-Phase (Mitosis / Spindle): Microtubule Inhibitors (Vincristine, Paclitaxel) G2-Phase: Bleomycin
⛓️ 2. Cell-Cycle Non-Specific (CCNS) Cytotoxic Agents
DNA Cross-Linkers: Alkylating Agents (Cyclophosphamide, Cisplatin) DNA Intercalators: Antitumor Antibiotics (Doxorubicin)
🎯 3. Targeted & Biological Therapies
Surface Antigen Antibodies: Monoclonal Antibodies (Rituximab - CD20, Trastuzumab - HER2) Intracellular Enzyme Blockers: Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors (Imatinib - BCR-ABL) Hormone Signal Modulators: Hormones & Antagonists (Tamoxifen - ER Antagonist)
