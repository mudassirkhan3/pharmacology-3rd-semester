📊 Master Exam Summary Table (PK, PD & UHS Unit I Content)

Comprehensive, high-yield comparative breakdown combining Pharmacokinetics, Pharmacodynamics, and mandatory UHS Unit I examination targets.

Domain / Parameter Pharmacokinetics (PK) Pharmacodynamics (PD) UHS Unit I Essential Content
1. Core Definition & Scope PK Core
"What body does to drug" — Movement of drug across biological membranes (ADME) over time.		 PD Core
"What drug does to body" — Biochemical, cellular, and organ response mechanisms.		 UHS Target
• Core Terms: Posology, Toxicology, Pharmacotherapeutics, Pharmacy.
• History: Avicenna, Paracelsus, Sertürner.
2. Core Processes & Mechanisms ADME Pathway
• Absorption (Bioavailability 'F')
• Distribution (Vd, Albumin binding)
• Metabolism (CYP450 Phase I/II)
• Excretion (Clearance, t1/2)		 Receptor Action
• Agonist, Antagonist, Partial Agonist
• Dose-Response Relationship
• Potency (EC50) vs. Efficacy (Emax)		 Sources & Naming
• Sources: Plant (Digoxin), Animal (Heparin), Microbe (Penicillin), Synthetic, rDNA.
• Names: Chemical, Generic, Trade/Brand.
3. Key Formulas & Parameters Formulas
• Bioavailability F (%)
• Vd = Dose / C0
• t1/2 = (0.693 × Vd) / Clearance
• Steady State = 4 to 5 × t1/2		 Safety Indices
• Therapeutic Index: TI = TD50 / ED50
• Margin of Safety
• Receptor Occupancy Model		 Therapeutic Purposes
• Diagnostic, Curative, Symptomatic / Palliative, Prophylactic, Replacement, Supportive.
• Official Books: Pharmacopeias (USP, BP, IP).
4. Clinical Safety & High-Alert Risks Risk Factors
• Hepatic First-Pass Extraction
• Hypoalbuminemia → Toxicity
• Renal Failure → Drug Accumulation		 TDM Warning
• Narrow Therapeutic Index (Digoxin, Lithium, Warfarin, Gentamicin)
• Desensitization / Tolerance		 Hospital Systems
• Distribution: Floor Stock, Individual Orders, Unit Dose System (Safest).
• Schedules I–V & DRAP Act 1976.
5. Priority Nursing Responsibilities Nursing Care
• Monitor Renal/Liver function (eGFR, BUN, LFTs).
• Time Peak & Trough Blood Levels.		 Nursing Care
• Assess therapeutic efficacy.
• Monitor onset, peak, duration, and side effects/toxicity.		 Nursing Care
• 10 Rights of Drug Administration.
• Independent double-checks on high-alert drugs.
• Posology dosage calculations.
