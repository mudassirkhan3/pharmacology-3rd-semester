3. Potency vs. Efficacy Concepts

Understanding the difference between potency and efficacy is critical when evaluating therapeutic drug options.

⚡ Potency

Refers to the amount of drug needed to produce a specific biological effect. A more potent drug requires a smaller dose (shifts curve to the left).

🌟 Efficacy

Refers to the maximum therapeutic effect a drug can produce regardless of dosage. Higher efficacy means a higher maximal response ceiling.

4. Therapeutic Index (TI) & Safety Margin

The Therapeutic Index measures a drug's safety margin by comparing the dose that causes toxicity to the dose that produces the desired therapeutic effect.

📐 Therapeutic Index Formula

$$TI = \\frac{TD_{50}}{ED_{50}}$$

Where TD50 is the Toxic Dose for 50% of the population, and ED50 is the Effective Dose for 50% of the population. A wider margin indicates a safer drug.

📊 Therapeutic Window Curve (ED50 vs TD50)

Clinical Significance: Drugs with a narrow therapeutic index (e.g., Digoxin, Warfarin) require strict therapeutic drug monitoring (TDM) to prevent lethal toxicity.
