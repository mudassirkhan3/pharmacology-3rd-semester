Nursing Clinical Considerations

  • 1
    PPI Administration: Instruct patients to take Proton Pump Inhibitors 30 minutes before breakfast for maximum effectiveness.
  • 2
    Antacid Interactions: Antacids can alter the absorption of other medications; space doses at least 1–2 hours apart.
  • 3
    Misoprostol Warning: Contraindicated in pregnancy as it may stimulate uterine contractions. Screen patients accordingly.
  • 4
    H. Pylori Compliance: Stress completion of the full course of antimicrobials to prevent treatment failure and resistant bacterial strains.
