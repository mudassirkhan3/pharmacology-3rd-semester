Nursing Study Guide - 3rd Semester

🔗 Reference: Pharmacology 3rd Semester Classifications

1. Antimicrobial Agents (H. Pylori)

Clarithromycin: Inhibits protein synthesis.
Amoxicillin: Disrupts bacterial cell wall.
Metronidazole: Inhibits DNA synthesis.

Nursing Note: Always monitor for allergic reactions and diarrhea during triple therapy.

2. H2 Receptor Blockers

Famotidine: Potent, long-acting secretion inhibitor.
Cimetidine: High drug interaction profile.
Ranitidine: Competitive histamine antagonist.

3. Proton Pump Inhibitors & Prostaglandins

Omeprazole (PPI): Binds H+/K+ ATPase irreversibly.
Pantoprazole (PPI): High potency, available IV.
Misoprostol (PG): Enhances mucus/bicarb.

Nursing Note: Take PPIs 30 mins before breakfast. Misoprostol is contraindicated in pregnancy.

4. Antimuscarinics, Antacids & Mucosal Protective

Pirenzepine: M1-selective antimuscarinic.
Mg/Al Hydroxide: Rapid acid neutralization.
Sucralfate: Forms a physical ulcer protective barrier.
Bismuth Subsalicylate: Anti-secretory & coating agent.

Nursing Note: Sucralfate needs an acidic pH; do not give with antacids simultaneously.
