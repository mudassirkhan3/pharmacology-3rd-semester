Unit II: Infections
Antifungal (Triazole)

Fluconazole

Mechanism of Action (MOA):

Inhibits fungal cytochrome P450 sterol C-14 alpha-demethylation.

Clinical Uses:

Used for candidiasis, cryptococcal meningitis, and systemic fungal prophylaxis.

Common Side Effects:

Headache, nausea, abdominal pain, elevated liver enzymes.
