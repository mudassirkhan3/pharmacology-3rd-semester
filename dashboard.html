Unit II: Infections

Amoxicillin

Mechanism of Action (MOA):

Inhibits bacterial cell wall synthesis by binding to penicillin-binding proteins (PBPs), causing cell lysis.

Key Nursing Considerations:

Check for penicillin allergy before administration. Administer with food if GI upset occurs. Monitor for signs of superinfections (e.g. oral candidiasis, diarrhea).

Common Side Effects:

Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, mild rash, hypersensitivity reactions.
