Histamine H2-Receptor Blockers (H2RAs)

Competitive antagonists of histamine H2 receptors designed to suppress basal and meal-stimulated gastric acid secretion for the management of peptic ulcers, GERD, and stress ulcer prophylaxis.

3 Mechanism of Action (Detailed & Simple Explanations)

MOA

H2RAs suppress gastric acidity through selective competitive receptor antagonism at the parietal cell level:

H2 Receptor Blockade Cascade

💡 Simple Concept: Blocks histamine from ringing the parietal cell's "acid alarm bell", turning down internal power (cAMP) and stopping the acid pump.

1
Selective H2 Receptor Blockade: H2RAs reversibly and competitively bind to histamine H2 receptors located on the basolateral membrane of gastric parietal cells.
2
Intracellular cAMP Suppression: By blocking histamine stimulation, intracellular adenylate cyclase activity drops, lowering cyclic AMP (cAMP) signal levels.
3
Proton Pump Inactivation: Decreased cAMP inhibits activation of the H+/K+-ATPase proton pump, significantly reducing gastric acid volume and concentration (especially nocturnal acid output).

4 Clinical Uses & Therapeutic Indications

Indications
01 Peptic Ulcer Disease (Gastric and Duodenal Ulcers)
02 Mild to Moderate Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD)
03 Stress Ulcer Prophylaxis in critically ill hospitalized patients
04 Aspiration Pneumonitis Prophylaxis (pre-anesthesia adjunct)

6 Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs)

Side Effects
1. Central Nervous System Effects

Headache, dizziness, fatigue, and confusion or hallucinations (more common in elderly patients or those with renal impairment).

2. Antiandrogenic Effects (Cimetidine Specific)

Gynecomastia, reduced libido, and impotence due to blockade of androgen receptors.

3. Gastrointestinal & Hematologic

Diarrhea or constipation, alongside rare instances of thrombocytopenia or neutropenia.

7 ADR Memory Trick

Mnemonic
ADR Mnemonic: "C.A.D."
• CNS toxicity in elderly/renal
• Antiandrogenic side effects (Cimetidine)
• Digestive changes & headache

8 Pharmacokinetics

PK Profiles
💊 Administration & Renal Excretion

Available in oral, oral liquid, and IV/IM formats. Primarily eliminated via renal clearance; dose adjustments are mandatory in renal impairment.

⚙️ Hepatic Metabolism

Famotidine undergoes minimal liver metabolism, whereas Cimetidine heavily inhibits hepatic CYP450 enzyme pathways.

9 Key Nursing Takeaways & Patient Safety

Patient Safety
1. Monitor Renal Function

Check baseline BUN and serum creatinine, and ensure appropriate dosage reductions are ordered for patients with renal impairment.

2. Assess for CNS Toxicity in Older Adults

Be vigilant for signs of confusion, agitation, or disorientation, especially in elderly hospitalized patients receiving IV H2RAs.

3. Review Medication Reconciliation

If administering Cimetidine, closely monitor for interactions with narrow-index drugs like warfarin or phenytoin due to CYP450 inhibition.

4. Timing & Antacid Separation

Administer before meals or at bedtime, and separate antacid administration by at least 1 hour to prevent impaired H2RA absorption.
