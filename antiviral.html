1 Respiratory Virus Antivirals (Influenza, RSV, COVID-19)

Respiratory

Medications used to treat or prevent respiratory viral infections by stopping uncoating, gene copying, or viral release.

Prototype Drugs (Bullet Form):

  • Oseltamivir (Tamiflu)
  • Zanamivir
  • Peramivir

MOA: Prevents viral detachment from host cell membrane by inhibiting viral neuraminidase. Keeps new flu viruses trapped inside the infected cell.

Uses: Treatment and prevention of acute Influenza A and B (must give within 48 hours of symptoms!).

ADRs: Nausea, vomiting, delirium/hallucinations, and bronchospasm (avoid inhaled zanamivir in asthma/COPD!).

Prototype Drugs (Bullet Form):

  • Baloxavir Marboxil

MOA: Blocks viral gene transcription early by inhibiting cap-dependent endonuclease inside the viral polymerase complex.

Uses: Single-dose oral treatment for acute uncomplicated flu (ages 12+).

ADRs: Diarrhea, bronchitis, headache, and nausea.

Prototype Drugs (Bullet Form):

  • Amantadine
  • Rimantadine

MOA: Blocks M2 proton channels on Influenza A to prevent viral uncoating in host cells.

Uses: Historical treatment of Influenza A (rarely used now due to widespread resistance).

ADRs: CNS dizziness, insomnia, confusion, and peripheral anticholinergic symptoms (dry mouth).

2 HIV Reverse Transcriptase Inhibitors (NRTIs vs. NtRTIs)

HIV Core

These drugs act as fake DNA building blocks that incorporate into viral DNA and immediately break off DNA chain elongation.

Prototype Drugs (Bullet Form):

  • Zidovudine (AZT)
  • Lamivudine (3TC)
  • Emtricitabine (FTC)
  • Abacavir (ABC)

MOA: Synthetic nucleosides requiring 3 intracellular phosphorylation steps to stop reverse transcriptase.

Key Exam Points: Abacavir requires mandatory genetic screening for HLA-B*5701 before starting (risk of fatal hypersensitivity!). Zidovudine causes severe macrocytic anemia and bone marrow suppression.

ADRs: Lactic acidosis, hepatic steatosis, headache, GI upset.

Prototype Drugs (Bullet Form):

  • Tenofovir Disoproxil Fumarate (TDF)
  • Tenofovir Alafenamide (TAF)

MOA: Possesses a pre-attached phosphonate group (nucleotide), so it needs only 2 phosphorylation steps to activate!

Key Exam Points: TDF carries a higher risk of renal proximal tubule failure (Fanconi syndrome) and bone mineral density loss. TAF is a newer formulation with lower kidney/bone toxicity.

3 Hepatitis B & C Antivirals (DAAs)

Hepatitis

Specialized agents targeting chronic hepatitis infections, including direct-acting antivirals (DAAs) that achieve high cure rates for HCV.

Prototype Drugs (Bullet Form):

  • Entecavir
  • Tenofovir (TDF / TAF)
  • Adefovir Dipivoxil

MOA: Inhibits HBV DNA polymerase base priming and DNA synthesis.

ADRs: Nephrotoxicity, lactic acidosis, and elevated creatine kinase.

Prototype Drugs (Bullet Form):

  • Sofosbuvir (-buvir)
  • Ledipasvir / Velpatasvir (-asvir)
  • Glecaprevir (-previr)

MOA Name Endings: -buvir (polymerase), -asvir (NS5A complex), -previr (protease).

Nursing Warning: Do NOT combine Sofosbuvir with Amiodarone due to risk of fatal severe bradycardia!

4 Herpesvirus (HSV/VZV) & Cytomegalovirus (CMV) Drugs

Herpes/CMV

Agents that block viral DNA polymerase in Herpes Simplex, Shingles, and Cytomegalovirus infections.

Prototype Drugs (Bullet Form):

  • Acyclovir
  • Valacyclovir
  • Famciclovir

MOA: Requires viral Thymidine Kinase to activate initial monophosphate form, then cellular enzymes form triphosphate to stop viral DNA polymerase.

Nursing Warning: IV Acyclovir causes Crystalline Nephropathy! Always infuse with aggressive IV saline hydration!

Prototype Drugs (Bullet Form):

  • Ganciclovir / Valganciclovir
  • Foscarnet & Cidofovir

MOA & ADRs: Ganciclovir requires viral UL97 kinase and causes severe bone marrow suppression. Foscarnet/Cidofovir bypass kinase activation but cause severe nephrotoxicity and electrolyte wasting.

5 HIV NNRTIs, Protease Inhibitors & INSTIs

Advanced HIV

Target non-nucleoside binding sites, viral protein cleavage, or integration into human chromosomes.

Prototype Drugs (Bullet Form):

  • Dolutegravir
  • Bictegravir
  • Raltegravir

MOA: Chelates magnesium metal ions at the active site of HIV integrase, stopping proviral DNA strand transfer into human host chromosomes.

Uses: Preferred frontline anchor class for HIV treatment.

Prototype Drugs (Bullet Form):

  • Darunavir, Atazanavir (-navir)
  • Efavirenz, Rilpivirine (NNRTIs)

PI MOA & ADRs: Inhibit HIV protease from chopping polyproteins, yielding non-infectious virions. Associated with hyperglycemia, lipodystrophy, and dyslipidemia.

6 Critical Nursing Exam Warnings

Exam Safety
1. Hepatitis B Reactivation Alert

Always test patients for Hepatitis B before starting or stopping HIV regimens containing Tenofovir, Lamivudine, or Emtricitabine.

2. Abacavir Screening

Mandatory genetic screening for HLA-B*5701 before dispensing Abacavir to prevent fatal hypersensitivity.

3. Acyclovir Hydration

Run IV saline alongside IV Acyclovir to prevent crystalline nephropathy and acute kidney injury.

4. Amiodarone & Sofosbuvir Interaction

Never co-administer Sofosbuvir with Amiodarone due to severe, potentially fatal bradycardia.
