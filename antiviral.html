Antiviral Pharmacology Guide — Expanded Classifications & HIV NRTIs

Expanded reference guide featuring comprehensive drug listings, dedicated HIV Nucleoside and Nucleotide Reverse Transcriptase Inhibitors (NRTIs/NtRTIs), short MOAs, Uses, and ADRs.

Classification: Drugs for Respiratory Virus Infections

Expanded pharmacological breakdown of antiviral agents targeting influenza, RSV, and coronavirus infections.

📂 Drug Inventory: Short MOA, Uses & ADRs

Oseltamivir / Zanamivir / Peramivir
Short MOA: Inhibits viral neuraminidase enzyme, preventing viral release from host cells.
Uses: Treatment and chemoprophylaxis of acute uncomplicated Influenza A and B.
ADRs: Nausea, vomiting, headache, psychiatric disturbances (hallucinations), bronchospasm (inhaled zanamivir).
Baloxavir Marboxil
Short MOA: Blocks cap-dependent endonuclease activity, halting viral messenger RNA transcription.
Uses: Single-dose treatment of acute uncomplicated influenza in patients aged 12 and older.
ADRs: Diarrhea, bronchitis, nausea, nasopharyngitis, headache.
Amantadine / Rimantadine
Short MOA: Inhibits the M2 proton ion channel, preventing viral uncoating (Influenza A).
Uses: Historical treatment and prophylaxis of Influenza A (largely abandoned due to high viral resistance).
ADRs: CNS toxicity (nervousness, anxiety, insomnia, dizziness), anticholinergic effects (dry mouth, blurred vision).
Palivizumab
Short MOA: Monoclonal antibody binding to RSV F protein epitope to block cellular entry.
Uses: Prevention of serious lower respiratory tract disease caused by RSV in high-risk infants.
ADRs: Injection site reactions, upper respiratory infection, fever, rash.
Remdesivir
Short MOA: Nucleotide analog inhibitor of viral RNA-dependent RNA polymerase.
Uses: Treatment of COVID-19 requiring supplemental oxygen.
ADRs: Elevated liver transaminases (ALT/AST), nausea, infusion-related hypersensitivity reactions.

💡 Mnemonic: "O.B.A.P.R."

Oseltamivir • Baloxavir • Amantadine • Palivizumab • Remdesivir.

Classification: HIV Nucleoside & Nucleotide Reverse Transcriptase Inhibitors (NRTIs & NtRTIs)

Backbone agents of antiretroviral therapy (ART). They act as false building blocks that incorporate into viral DNA and cause chain termination.

📂 Drug Inventory: Short MOA, Uses & ADRs

Zidovudine (AZT / ZDV)
Short MOA: Thymidine analog NRTI that terminates viral DNA chain elongation.
Uses: HIV-1 treatment and prevention of perinatal (mother-to-child) transmission.
ADRs: Severe bone marrow suppression (anemia, neutropenia), macrocytic anemia, headache, myopathy, GI upset.
Lamivudine (3TC)
Short MOA: Cytidine analog NRTI inhibiting HIV reverse transcriptase.
Uses: Comprehensive cART regimens for HIV and dual-activity against chronic Hepatitis B.
ADRs: Minimal toxicity profile; headache, fatigue, nausea, occasional pancreatitis.
Emtricitabine (FTC)
Short MOA: Fluorinated cytidine analog NRTI inhibiting reverse transcription.
Uses: First-line HIV treatment and Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis (PrEP).
ADRs: Headache, diarrhea, nausea, benign skin hyperpigmentation (especially on palms/soles).
Abacavir (ABC)
Short MOA: Guanosine analog NRTI causing viral DNA chain termination.
Uses: Frontline cART backbone (requires mandatory pre-screening for HLA-B*5701 allele).
ADRs: Fatal hypersensitivity reaction (fever, rash, GI symptoms in HLA-B*5701 positive), nausea, insomnia.
Tenofovir Disoproxil Fumarate (TDF)
Short MOA: Nucleotide analog (NtRTI) mimicking adenosine monophosphate to block reverse transcriptase.
Uses: HIV treatment, PrEP, and chronic Hepatitis B therapy.
ADRs: Nephrotoxicity, proximal renal tubular dysfunction (Fanconi syndrome), decreased bone mineral density.
Tenofovir Alafenamide (TAF)
Short MOA: Targeted nucleotide analog (NtRTI) engineered to maximize intracellular delivery in lymphoid cells and hepatocytes.
Uses: HIV treatment and PrEP with significantly reduced renal and bone toxicities compared to TDF.
ADRs: Headache, fatigue, nausea; far lower rates of renal impairment and bone density loss.
Stavudine (d4T)
Short MOA: Thymidine analog NRTI terminating viral DNA synthesis.
Uses: Historical HIV treatment (rarely used today due to high toxicity profile).
ADRs: Severe peripheral neuropathy, lipoatrophy (fat wasting), lactic acidosis, pancreatitis.
Didanosine (ddI)
Short MOA: Purine analog NRTI inhibiting HIV reverse transcriptase.
Uses: Historical alternative HIV treatment.
ADRs: Dose-dependent pancreatitis, peripheral neuropathy, diarrhea, lactic acidosis.

💡 Mnemonic: "Z.L.E.A.T.S.D." (HIV NRTIs & NtRTIs)

Zidovudine • Lamivudine • Emtricitabine • Abacavir • Tenofovir (TDF/TAF) • Stavudine • Didanosine.

Classification: Drugs for Hepatitis B Virus (HBV) Infections

Expanded pharmacological agents dedicated to chronic Hepatitis B management.

📂 Drug Inventory: Short MOA, Uses & ADRs

Entecavir
Short MOA: Guanosine nucleoside analog competing with natural substrate to inhibit HBV polymerase.
Uses: Chronic hepatitis B with active viral replication.
ADRs: Headache, fatigue, dizziness, nausea, rare lactic acidosis.
Telbivudine
Short MOA: Natural L-nucleoside analog inhibiting HBV DNA polymerase.
Uses: Chronic hepatitis B infection with high viral load.
ADRs: Elevated creatine kinase (CK) levels, myopathy, fatigue, headache, dizziness.
Adefovir Dipivoxil
Short MOA: Acyclic nucleotide analog inhibiting HBV DNA polymerase.
Uses: Chronic hepatitis B, including lamivudine-resistant strains.
ADRs: Dose-dependent nephrotoxicity, hypophosphatemia, asthenia, headache.
Peginterferon Alfa-2a
Short MOA: Pegylated cytokine stimulating host cell-mediated immune response against HBV.
Uses: HBeAg-positive and HBeAg-negative chronic hepatitis B.
ADRs: Flu-like symptoms, severe depression, bone marrow suppression, alopecia, thyroid disorders.

💡 Mnemonic: "E.T.A.P." (HBV Agents)

Entecavir • Telbivudine • Adefovir • Peginterferon.

Classification: Direct-Acting Antivirals (DAAs) for Hepatitis C Virus (HCV)

Expanded direct-acting antiviral drug classes targeting viral non-structural proteins.

📂 Drug Inventory: Short MOA, Uses & ADRs

Sofosbuvir
Short MOA: Nucleotide analog inhibitor of NS5B RNA-dependent RNA polymerase.
Uses: Chronic HCV genotypes 1 through 6 in combination regimens.
ADRs: Fatigue, headache, insomnia, severe symptomatic bradycardia if co-administered with amiodarone.
Ledipasvir / Velpatasvir / Pibrentasvir / Daclatasvir
Short MOA: Inhibits NS5A viral phosphoprotein required for viral replication complex assembly.
Uses: Pan-genotypic chronic hepatitis C virus eradication.
ADRs: Headache, fatigue, diarrhea, nausea, dizziness.
Glecaprevir / Voxilaprevir / Simeprevir / Grazoprevir
Short MOA: Inhibits NS3/4A serine proteases needed for viral polyprotein cleavage.
Uses: Treatment of chronic HCV including DAA-failure cases and compensated cirrhosis.
ADRs: Fatigue, headache, nausea, pruritus, transient serum bilirubin elevations, photosensitivity (simeprevir).
Ribavirin
Short MOA: Guanosine analog enhancing viral mutation rates and depleting intracellular pools.
Uses: Combination regimens for chronic HCV or severe RSV.
ADRs: Hemolytic anemia, severe teratogenicity (Category X), fatigue, rash.

💡 Mnemonic: "S.L.G.R."

Sofosbuvir • Ledipasvir • Glecaprevir • Ribavirin.

Classification: Drugs for Herpesvirus and Cytomegalovirus (CMV)

Expanded nucleoside and nucleotide analogs for HSV, VZV, and CMV infections.

📂 Drug Inventory: Short MOA, Uses & ADRs

Acyclovir / Valacyclovir / Famciclovir
Short MOA: Viral DNA polymerase inhibitor activated by viral thymidine kinase.
Uses: Genital herpes, shingles (VZV), herpes encephalitis.
ADRs: Nausea, headache, crystalline nephropathy and neurotoxicity (with rapid IV bolus).
Ganciclovir / Valganciclovir
Short MOA: Specialized nucleoside analog inhibiting CMV DNA polymerase.
Uses: Treatment and prevention of CMV retinitis in immunocompromised patients.
ADRs: Severe bone marrow suppression (leukopenia, thrombocytopenia), fever, rash.
Cidofovir
Short MOA: Nucleotide analog inhibiting viral DNA polymerase directly.
Uses: Resistant CMV retinitis in AIDS patients.
ADRs: Severe nephrotoxicity (requires probenecid/saline hydration), proteinuria, neutropenia.
Foscarnet
Short MOA: Pyrophosphate analog inhibiting viral DNA polymerase binding site.
Uses: Acyclovir-resistant HSV/VZV and ganciclovir-resistant CMV.
ADRs: Nephrotoxicity, severe electrolyte disturbances (hypocalcemia, hypomagnesemia), seizures.
Letermovir
Short MOA: Inhibits CMV terminase complex, blocking viral DNA packaging.
Uses: Prophylaxis of CMV infection in allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplant recipients.
ADRs: Nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, peripheral edema, headache, fatigue.

💡 Mnemonic: "A.G.C.F.L."

Acyclovir • Ganciclovir • Cidofovir • Foscarnet • Letermovir.

Classification: HIV Antiretrovirals (NNRTIs, Protease Inhibitors & Entry/Capsid Inhibitors)

Expanded classes targeting non-nucleoside reverse transcriptase, proteases, viral entry, and capsid assembly.

📂 Drug Inventory: Short MOA, Uses & ADRs

Efavirenz / Rilpivirine / Doravirine / Nevirapine / Etravirine
Short MOA: Non-competitively binds reverse transcriptase to induce a conformational halt.
Uses: Combination antiretroviral therapy (cART) for HIV-1 infection.
ADRs: CNS symptoms (vivid dreams, dizziness with efavirenz), severe rash, hepatotoxicity (nevirapine).
Darunavir / Atazanavir / Lopinavir / Indinavir / Saquinavir / Tipranavir
Short MOA: Binds HIV protease active site to prevent viral polyprotein cleavage into mature proteins.
Uses: Treatment-naive and experienced HIV-1 infection management.
ADRs: Dyslipidemia, insulin resistance, lipodystrophy, indirect hyperbilirubinemia (atazanavir), nephrolithiasis (indinavir).
Maraviroc
Short MOA: CCR5 coreceptor antagonist blocking viral attachment to CD4 cells.
Uses: Treatment-experienced adults infected with CCR5-tropic HIV-1 strains.
ADRs: Hepatotoxicity (preceded by allergic rash), upper respiratory infections, orthostatic hypotension.
Enfuvirtide
Short MOA: Fusion inhibitor preventing gp41 conformational changes required for viral-host membrane fusion.
Uses: Salvage therapy for heavily treatment-experienced HIV patients.
ADRs: Painful subcutaneous injection site nodules and erythema, increased bacterial pneumonia risk.
Fostemsavir / Ibalizumab / Lenacapavir
Short MOA: Novel attachment (gp120), post-attachment (CD4 receptor), and capsid protein inhibitors.
Uses: Multidrug-resistant advanced HIV-1 salvage regimens.
ADRs: Nausea, diarrhea, elevated liver enzymes, injection site reactions (lenacapavir).

💡 Mnemonic: "E.D.M.E.F."

Efavirenz • Darunavir • Maraviroc • Enfuvirtide • Fostemsavir.

Classification: HIV Integrase Strand Transfer Inhibitors (INSTIs)

Expanded frontline anchor class in modern antiretroviral therapy regimens.

📂 Drug Inventory: Short MOA, Uses & ADRs

Dolutegravir / Bictegravir
Short MOA: Chelates active metals in integrase to block viral DNA strand transfer.
Uses: Preferred frontline anchor agents for HIV-1 initial and maintenance treatment.
ADRs: Insomnia, headache, weight gain, nausea.
Raltegravir
Short MOA: Inhibits viral DNA insertion into human host cell chromosomes.
Uses: Treatment of HIV-1 infection in combination regimens.
ADRs: Insomnia, nausea, headache, diarrhea, severe hypersensitivity (DRESS syndrome), CPK elevations.
Elvitegravir
Short MOA: Integrase strand transfer inhibition requiring pharmacokinetic boosting.
Uses: Combined single-tablet regimens for HIV-1 management.
ADRs: Nausea, diarrhea, headache, insomnia.
Cabotegravir
Short MOA: Long-acting integrase strand transfer inhibitor.
Uses: Long-acting injectable HIV treatment and pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP).
ADRs: Injection site reactions (pain, nodules), pyrexia, fatigue, headache, musculoskeletal pain.

💡 Mnemonic: Ending in "-tegravir"

Bictegravir • Cabotegravir • Dolutegravir • Elvitegravir • Raltegravir.

Classification: Pharmacokinetic Enhancers for HIV (Boosters)

Agents used to inhibit CYP3A4 enzymes and boost co-administered antiretroviral drug half-lives.

📂 Drug Inventory: Short MOA, Uses & ADRs

Ritonavir
Short MOA: Potent CYP3A4 inhibitor used at low doses to boost protease half-lives.
Uses: Low-dose pharmacokinetic enhancer for protease inhibitors.
ADRs: GI distress (nausea, diarrhea), dyslipidemia, elevated transaminases.
Cobicistat
Short MOA: Selective CYP3A enzyme inhibitor with zero intrinsic anti-HIV activity.
Uses: Pharmacokinetic booster in fixed-dose single-tablet regimens.
ADRs: Benign increase in serum creatinine (due to tubular secretion inhibition), nausea, jaundice.

💡 Mnemonic: "R.C. Boosters"

Ritonavir • Cobicistat.

Classification: HIV Fixed-Dose Combinations (Single-Tablet Regimens)

Expanded single-tablet regimens combining multiple antiretroviral classes into one daily pill.

📂 Regimen Inventory: Short MOA, Uses & ADRs

Biktarvy (Bictegravir / Emtricitabine / TAF)
Short MOA: Multitarget synergy (INSTI + 2 NRTIs) blocking integration and reverse transcription.
Uses: Frontline complete single-tablet regimen for HIV-1 treatment-naive adults.
ADRs: Diarrhea, nausea, headache, mild lipid changes; minimal renal/bone risk.
Triumeq (Dolutegravir / Abacavir / Lamivudine)
Short MOA: Triple-drug blockade via integrase and reverse transcriptase inhibition.
Uses: Complete ART regimen (requires negative HLA-B*5701 screening).
ADRs: Insomnia, fatigue, nausea, headache, severe hypersensitivity if genetically susceptible.
Truvada / Descovy (NRTI Backbone Combinations)
Short MOA: Dual NRTI combinations preventing viral DNA chain elongation.
Uses: Foundation for HIV cART regimens and Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis (PrEP).
ADRs: Headache, nausea, GI distress; renal/bone monitoring needed for Truvada (TDF).
Symtuza (Darunavir / Cobicistat / Emtricitabine / TAF)
Short MOA: Protease inhibition boosted by cobicistat paired with dual NRTIs.
Uses: Complete single-tablet regimen for HIV-1 treatment.
ADRs: Diarrhea, rash, nausea, headache, dyslipidemia, insulin resistance.

🚫 Clinical Warnings & Nursing Interventions

Always perform baseline hepatitis B screening and check renal function (eGFR) before administering tenofovir-containing regimens. Screen patients genetically for HLA-B*5701 before prescribing abacavir to prevent fatal hypersensitivity reactions.
