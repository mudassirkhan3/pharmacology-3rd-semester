General Nursing Measures & Patient Education

  • 1
    Culture & Sensitivity Testing: Always obtain diagnostic cultures (blood, urine, or wound) before administering the first dose of anti-infective therapy.
  • 2
    Complete Full Regimen: Emphasize to patients the critical importance of finishing the entire prescribed regimen to prevent antimicrobial resistance.
  • 3
    Hypersensitivity Screening: Screen for cross-sensitivities (e.g., Penicillins and Cephalosporins) and monitor patients during the first 30 minutes of administration.
  • 4
    Therapeutic Drug Monitoring (TDM): Regularly track serum peak and trough levels for narrow therapeutic index drugs like Vancomycin and Gentamicin.
