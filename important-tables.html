🫁 Chapter 32: Second-Line Antitubercular Agents

Characteristics, toxicities, and monitoring parameters for second-line tuberculosis drugs.

Drug Class / Agent Adverse Effects Clinical Comments & Monitoring
Fluoroquinolones GI intolerance, tendonitis, CNS toxicity, caffeine-like effects. Monitor LFTs, serum creatinine/BUN, and QT interval. Avoid concurrent ingestion with di- or trivalent cations (antacids/multivitamins).
Aminoglycosides & Capreomycin Nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity (vestibular and auditory). Not available orally. Requires serial monitoring of renal function, vestibular function, and hearing.
Macrolides GI intolerance, tinnitus. Monitor LFTs, serum creatinine/BUN, and QT interval. Watch for drug interactions due to CYP inhibition.
Ethionamide & PAS GI intolerance, hepatotoxicity, hypothyroidism. Monitor LFTs and TSH. High incidence of GI distress. Patients with G6PD deficiency face increased risk of hemolytic anemia with PAS.
Cycloserine Severe CNS toxicity (depression, anxiety, confusion, seizures). Requires strict psychiatric monitoring. Exacerbates seizures in epileptic patients. Monitor serum creatinine.
