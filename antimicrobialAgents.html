1 Respiratory Viruses (Influenza & COVID-19)

Respiratory

Target viral entry, uncoating, replication, or cellular release in upper and lower respiratory tract infections.

Prototype Drugs:

Oseltamivir, Zanamivir (Neuraminidase Inhibitors); Baloxavir Marboxil (Endonuclease Inhibitor); Remdesivir (RNA Polymerase Inhibitor).

💡 Mnemonic for Drug Classification: "Flu Gets Out Fast"

Flu (Influenza) | Gets (Baloxavir) | Out (Oseltamivir) | Fast (Remdesivir for COVID)

💊 Prodrug Mnemonic: "B = Brought as Pro"

Baloxavir marboxil is a oral ester prodrug converted in vivo to active baloxavir.

2 Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV)

HIV Therapeutics

Comprehensive combination antiretroviral therapy (cART) targeting reverse transcription, viral integration, and protein cleavage.

Prototype Drugs & Abbreviations:

Abacavir (ABC - NRTI), Tenofovir Alafenamide (TAF / TDF - NtRTI), Dolutegravir (DTG - INSTI), Darunavir (DRV - PI).

💡 Mnemonic for Drug Classification: "Always Try Doing Daily Care"

Abacavir | Tenofovir | Dolutegravir | Darunavir (Core active drugs in HIV treatment schemas)

💊 Prodrug Mnemonic: "TAF = Targeted Active Formula"

TAF (Tenofovir Alafenamide) is a smart prodrug designed to deliver active tenofovir directly into cells while sparing kidneys.

3 Hepatitis B Virus (HBV)

Hepatitis B

Target chronic HBV polymerase replication and modulate host immune response to suppress viral load and prevent cirrhosis.

Prototype Drugs & Abbreviations:

Entecavir (ETV), Peginterferon Alfa-2a (PEG-IFN), Adefovir dipivoxil.

💡 Mnemonic for Drug Classification: "Every Patient Heals"

Entecavir | Peginterferon | Hep B controlled!

💊 Prodrug Mnemonic: "Adefovir Dipivoxil = Absorbed Deeply"

Adefovir dipivoxil is an oral ester prodrug that enhances gastrointestinal absorption.

4 Hepatitis C Virus (HCV) Direct-Acting Antivirals

Hepatitis C

Target non-structural (NS) viral proteins to achieve permanent virologic cure (SVR > 95%) without interferon.

Prototype Drugs & Abbreviations:

Sofosbuvir (SOF), Ribavirin (RBV). Suffixes: -previr (Protease), -asvir (NS5A), -buvir (Polymerase).

💡 Mnemonic for Drug Classification: "Super Cure for Hep C"

Sofosbuvir | Curing | Hep C effectively!

💊 Prodrug Mnemonic: "Sofosbuvir = Safe Oral Formula"

Sofosbuvir is a nucleotide prodrug that undergoes intracellular liver metabolism.

5 Herpesvirus (HSV/VZV) & Cytomegalovirus (CMV)

Herpes / CMV

Nucleoside analogues requiring viral or cellular kinase activation to inhibit viral DNA polymerase.

Prototype Drugs & Abbreviations:

Acyclovir (ACV), Valacyclovir, Ganciclovir (GCV), Valganciclovir, Foscarnet.

💡 Mnemonic for Drug Classification: "Always Guard Cold Sores"

Acyclovir | Ganciclovir | Combat cold sores, shingles, and cytomegalovirus!

💊 Prodrug Mnemonic: "VAL = Viable Absorption Layer"

Valacyclovir & Valganciclovir are L-valyl ester prodrugs engineered for exceptional oral GI tract absorption.
