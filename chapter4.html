General Nursing Measures

  • 1
    Monitoring Coagulation: Regularly check lab values (PT/INR for Warfarin; aPTT for Heparin) before administering doses.
  • 2
    Bleeding Precautions: Implement strict bleeding precautions: use a soft-bristle toothbrush, electric razor, and minimize trauma.
  • 3
    Antidote Readiness: Always verify that reversal agents (Vitamin K, Protamine Sulfate) are readily available for emergency use.
  • 4
    Injection Safety: Avoid IM injections for patients on anticoagulants to prevent hematoma formation at the site.
