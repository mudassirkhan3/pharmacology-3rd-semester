💊 Antiparasitic Pharmacology

1. Antiprotozoals

Drug Enters Parasite
↓
Reduced to Toxic Radicals
↓
DNA Strand Breaks
↓
Parasite Replication Death

Mechanism Explained: These drugs act as "Trojan Horses." The drug enters the protozoan cell harmlessly, but once inside, the parasite's internal enzymes convert the drug into highly toxic reactive radicals. These radicals then aggressively attack the parasite's DNA, causing permanent strand fragmentation. Unable to repair its genetic code, the parasite cannot synthesize proteins or replicate, leading to rapid cell death.

Agents: Metronidazole, Chloroquine, Atovaquone.
Uses: Malaria, Giardiasis, Amoebic Dysentery, Trichomoniasis.
💡 Mnemonic: METAL (Metallic taste, Epigastric distress, Toxic to CNS, Abdominal cramps, Leukopenia).

2. Antihelminthics

Binding to Tubulin/Nerves
↓
Glucose Absorption Block
↓
Flaccid Paralysis
↓
Expulsion via Peristalsis

Mechanism Explained: These agents focus on structural and metabolic starvation. They bind to the parasite's microtubules (tubulin) or critical nerve receptors. By blocking the worm's ability to absorb glucose—their primary energy source—the worm becomes depleted of ATP. Simultaneously, nerve signaling is inhibited, causing total flaccid paralysis. Once immobilized, the worm can no longer anchor itself to the intestinal wall and is naturally eliminated by the host's normal bowel movements.

Agents: Albendazole, Ivermectin, Praziquantel.
Uses: Pinworms, Roundworms, Tapeworms, Strongyloidiasis.
💡 Mnemonic: WORMS (Whipworms, Oxyuriasis, Roundworms, Migrating larvae, Stapeworms).

3. Ectoparasiticides

Topical Absorption into Shell
↓
Sodium Channel Lock
↓
Continuous Nerve Firing
↓
Death by Paralysis

Mechanism Explained: These are surface-acting neurotoxins. When applied topically, the drug penetrates the chitinous exoskeleton of lice or mites. It specifically targets the parasite's voltage-gated sodium channels. By keeping these channels locked open, the drug causes repetitive, uncontrollable nerve discharge. The parasite suffers from extreme over-stimulation, leading to exhaustion, paralysis, and death. It effectively turns the parasite's own nervous system against it.

Agents: Permethrin, Malathion, Spinosad.
Uses: Head lice, Body lice, Pubic lice, Scabies.
💡 Mnemonic: SKIN (Stinging, Keep out of eyes, Irritation, Numbness).
