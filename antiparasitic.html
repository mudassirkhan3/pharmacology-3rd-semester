💊 Antiparasitic Pharmacology

1. Antiprotozoals

⚙️ MOA Detailed Flow

1
Ingestion: Drug enters the protozoan cell.
2
Activation: (Metronidazole) is reduced by internal parasitic enzymes into reactive, toxic radicals.
3
Destruction: These radicals attack the DNA structure, creating breaks in the genetic code.
4
Result: Parasite cannot replicate and dies.
Mnemonic for ADRs: "M.E.T.A.L."
• Metallic taste | Epigastric distress | Toxic to CNS | Abdominal cramps | Leukopenia
Nursing Takeaways:
  • Alcohol Ban: Disulfiram-like reaction (Violent vomiting). Patient MUST avoid alcohol 48 hours post-treatment.
  • Dark Urine: Inform patient that red/brown urine is expected (harmless).

2. Antihelminthics

⚙️ MOA Detailed Flow

1
Binding: Drug attaches to the worm's structural proteins (microtubules) or specific nerve receptors (Chloride channels).
2
Disruption: (Albendazole) blocks glucose absorption; (Ivermectin) hyperpolarizes the nerve cells.
3
Paralysis/Starvation: The worm becomes immobile or depleted of energy.
4
Result: The parasite loses its grip on the intestinal wall and is expelled via peristalsis.
Mnemonic for Uses: "W.O.R.M.S."
• Whipworms | Oxyuriasis (Pinworms) | Roundworms | Migrating larvae | Stapeworms
Nursing Takeaways:
  • Family Hygiene: Pinworms are highly contagious. Treat the entire household.
  • Environment: Wash all linens/pajamas in hot water. Cut nails short to stop the fecal-oral cycle (scratching to mouth).
  • Mazzotti Reaction: Monitor for fever/rash (Immune reaction to the dying worm carcasses).

3. Ectoparasiticides

⚙️ MOA Detailed Flow

1
Application: Topical cream/lotion absorbed into the exoskeleton of the mite/lice.
2
Channel Blockade: Drug binds to the Sodium channels in the parasite's nerve cell membrane.
3
Repolarization Failure: Sodium channels stay open; nerves fire continuously without stopping.
4
Result: Parasite suffers from uncontrollable twitching, paralysis, and death.
Mnemonic for ADRs: "S.K.I.N."
• Stinging | Keep away from eyes | Irritation | Numbness
Nursing Takeaways:
  • Timing: Follow instructions EXACTLY. Leave the medication on for the prescribed time (e.g., 8 hours) before washing.
  • Fomites: Non-washables (like stuffed toys) must be bagged for 14 days. Lice cannot survive without a human host for long.
