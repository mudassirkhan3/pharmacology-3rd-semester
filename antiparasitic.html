💊 Antiparasitic Pharmacology

1. Antiprotozoals

Simple Explanation: These medications are designed to target single-celled protozoa like malaria or amoebas. They work by interfering with the parasite's vital metabolic pathways or damaging their DNA. By halting these processes, the parasite cannot reproduce or generate energy, leading to its death and clearing the infection from the host's body.

Common Drugs:
Metronidazole / Tinidazole
Chloroquine / Hydroxychloroquine
Pyrimethamine
Pentamidine
MOA Flow:
Ingestion of Drug ↓ Activation/Enzyme Interaction ↓ DNA Strand Break or Heme Toxicity ↓ Parasite Replication Failure/Death
Clinical Uses:

Used to treat Malaria, Amoebic dysentery, Giardiasis, Trichomoniasis, and Toxoplasmosis.

2. Antihelminthics

Simple Explanation: These drugs are specifically created to treat infestations caused by parasitic worms like pinworms and tapeworms. They function by either paralyzing the worms so they lose their grip on the host's intestine or by starving them by blocking nutrient absorption. Once the worm is immobile or weak, it is easily flushed out by normal bowel movements.

Common Drugs:
Albendazole / Mebendazole
Ivermectin
Praziquantel
Pyrantel Pamoate
MOA Flow:
Binding to Tubulin or Nerve Receptors ↓ Microtubule/Ion Channel Disruption ↓ Starvation or Flaccid Paralysis ↓ Expulsion by Peristalsis
Clinical Uses:

Used to treat Pinworms (Enterobius), Roundworms (Ascaris), Tapeworms, Hookworms, and Strongyloidiasis.

3. Ectoparasiticides

Simple Explanation: These are topical agents used to eliminate parasites living on the skin's surface, such as lice and scabies mites. They act as neurotoxins to the parasites by disrupting their nerve cells. This causes uncontrollable muscle twitching and paralysis, effectively killing the pest so they can be washed away from the skin or hair.

Common Drugs:
Permethrin
Malathion
Spinosad
Benzyl Alcohol
MOA Flow:
Topical Absorption into Exoskeleton ↓ Sodium Channel Over-stimulation ↓ Continuous Nerve Firing (Paralysis) ↓ Parasite Death
Clinical Uses:

Used to treat Pediculosis (Head/Body Lice), Pubic Lice (Crabs), and Scabies infestations.
