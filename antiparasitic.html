💊 Antiparasitic Pharmacology

1. Antiprotozoals

Target: Amoebas, Malaria, Giardia.

Primary Drug Metronidazole

Damages parasitic DNA.

Secondary Drug Chloroquine

Blocks heme detoxification.

Mnemonic for ADRs: "M.E.T.A.L."
• Metallic taste | Epigastric distress | Toxic to CNS | Abdominal cramps | Leukopenia
Nursing Action:
  • CRITICAL: No alcohol. A "Disulfiram-like reaction" occurs (severe vomiting).
  • Check baseline CBC (watch for leukopenia).
  • Advise patient that dark/reddish-brown urine is a harmless side effect.

2. Antihelminthics

Target: Worms (Pinworms, Roundworms, Tapeworms).

Primary Drug Albendazole

Starves the worm by stopping sugar intake.

Primary Drug Ivermectin

Paralyzes the worm.

Mnemonic for Uses: "W.O.R.M.S."
• Whipworms | Oxyuriasis (Pinworms) | Roundworms | Migrating larvae | Stapeworms
Nursing Action:
  • Family Treatement: The entire family must be treated, even if asymptomatic.
  • Hygiene is Key: Wash all sheets in hot water. Cut fingernails short (to prevent re-infection from scratching).
  • Mazzotti Reaction: Monitor for fever/rash caused by mass death of parasites.

3. Ectoparasiticides

Target: External parasites (Lice, Scabies).

Drug Permethrin

Neurotoxin that paralyzes external parasites.

Mnemonic for ADRs: "S.K.I.N."
• Stinging | Keep out of eyes | Irritation | Numbness
Nursing Action:
  • Proper Application: Leave the cream on for the full required time (e.g., 8–14 hours for scabies) before washing.
  • Cleaning: Bag up non-washable items (stuffed animals) for 14 days; parasites die without a human host.
Safety Warning: Always verify pregnancy status before administration. Some antiparasitics are harmful to the fetus.
