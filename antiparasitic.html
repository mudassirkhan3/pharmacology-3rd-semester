1. Antiprotozoals

⚡ MOA Workflow
1. Entry into Parasite
2. Reduced to Toxic Radicals
3. DNA Strand Fragmentation
4. Replication Death

Concept: These agents act as intracellular saboteurs. They enter single-celled protozoa, get activated into toxic chemical nitro-radicals, and cause irreversible DNA strand fragmentation.

Common Drugs & Features:
Metronidazole / TinidazoleFirst-line nitroimidazole for anaerobic bugs & protozoa.
Chloroquine / Hydroxy-Blocks heme detox in malaria plasmodium parasites.
AtovaquoneInhibits fungal & protozoal mitochondrial electron transport.

Clinical Uses:

• Malaria prophylaxis and acute attack treatment
• Giardiasis & Amoebic dysentery (stomach/gut infections)
• Trichomoniasis (STI - treat both partners)
• Toxoplasmosis in immunocompromised patients

💡 Adverse Effects Mnemonic: "M.E.T.A.L."

MMetallic taste in mouth IMP FOR EXAM
EEpigastric distress & nausea
TToxic to CNS (dizziness, ataxia, seizures)
AAbdominal cramping & dark urine
LLeukopenia (monitor CBC during prolonged courses)

🚫 Clinical Safety Warning IMP FOR EXAM

Disulfiram-like Reaction: Strictly instruct patients taking Metronidazole to avoid all alcohol during treatment and for at least 48 hours after to prevent severe flushing, vomiting, and tachycardia.

2. Antihelminthics

⚡ MOA Workflow
1. Bind Tubulin / Ion Nerves
2. Block Glucose Absorption
3. Flaccid Worm Paralysis
4. Expulsion by Peristalsis

Concept: These drugs focus on structural starvation and immobilization. By targeting the worm's internal microtubule network (tubulin) or hyperpolarizing nerve channels, they starve and paralyze parasitic worms for natural intestinal expulsion.

Common Drugs & Features:
Albendazole / MebendazoleInhibits microtubule assembly; broad spectrum. IMP FOR EXAM
IvermectinOpens GABA/glutamate-gated chloride channels causing paralysis.
PraziquantelIncreases calcium permeability causing worm muscle spasms.
Pyrantel PamoateDepolarizing neuromuscular blocker (OTC pinworm drug).

💡 Clinical Uses Mnemonic: "W.O.R.M.S."

WWhipworms (Trichuris trichiura)
OOxyuriasis / Pinworms (Enterobius vermicularis)
RRoundworms (Ascaris lumbricoides)
MMigrating larvae (Cutaneous larva migrans)
SStapeworms & Schistosomiasis (Flukes)

🚫 Clinical Safety Warning

Administer Albendazole with high-fat meals when treating tissue infections (like neurocysticercosis) to maximize systemic absorption. When treating luminal gut worms, give on an empty stomach.

3. Ectoparasiticides

⚡ MOA Workflow
1. Absorption into Exoskeleton
2. Sodium Channel Lock
3. Continuous Nerve Firing
4. Pest Paralysis & Death

Concept: Surface-acting neurotoxins that penetrate the hard chitin exoskeleton of lice and mites. By binding voltage-gated sodium channels, they cause non-stop neuronal depolarization, paralysis, and death of external parasites.

Common Drugs & Features:
Permethrin (5% Cream / 1% Lotion)First-line topical choice for Scabies and Head Lice.
MalathionOrganophosphate acetylcholinesterase inhibitor (flammable!).
SpinosadCauses neuronal hyperexcitation in lice nicotinic receptors.
Spinosad / Benzyl AlcoholAsphyxiates lice by blocking breathing spiracles.

Clinical Uses:

• Head & Body Lice (Pediculosis capitis / corporis)
• Pubic Lice ("Crabs" - Phthirus pubis)
• Scabies Mites (Sarcoptes scabiei infestation)

💡 Adverse Effects Mnemonic: "S.K.I.N."

SStinging, burning, and localized erythema
KKeep strictly out of eyes and mucous membranes
IIrritation and contact dermatitis
NNumbness or transient paresthesias at application site

🚫 Nursing Clinical Application Rules IMP FOR EXAM

Scabies Protocol: Apply 5% Permethrin cream from the neck down to the soles of the feet. Leave on for 8 to 14 hours before washing off. Treat all household contacts simultaneously and wash bedding in hot water.
