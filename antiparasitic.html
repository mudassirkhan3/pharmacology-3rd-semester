💊 Simple Antiparasitic Guide

1. Antiprotozoals

Used for: Malaria, Amoebas, and Giardia infections.

Common Drugs: Metronidazole, Tinidazole, Chloroquine, Paromomycin, Atovaquone.
Drug Ingested ⬇️ Activation inside parasite ⬇️ Toxic radicals break DNA ⬇️ Parasite dies

Simple Explanation: These drugs turn into "poison" inside the parasite. Once inside, they attack the parasite's genetic code (DNA), making it impossible for the parasite to grow or replicate.

ADR Mnemonic: "M.E.T.A.L."
• Metallic taste | Epigastric upset | Toxic to CNS | Abdominal cramps | Leukopenia
Nursing Takeaway: Never drink alcohol while taking Metronidazole (Violent vomiting!). Warn the patient their urine might turn reddish-brown.

2. Antihelminthics

Used for: Worms (Pinworms, Tapeworms, Roundworms).

Common Drugs: Albendazole, Mebendazole, Ivermectin, Praziquantel, Pyrantel Pamoate.
Drug enters worm ⬇️ Blocks glucose or nerve channels ⬇️ Starvation or Paralysis ⬇️ Expulsion from body

Simple Explanation: Worms need sugar to survive and muscles to hold onto your gut. These drugs either stop the worm from "eating" (starving them) or paralyze their muscles, so the body can flush them out.

Use Mnemonic: "W.O.R.M.S."
• Whipworms | Oxyuriasis | Roundworms | Migrating larvae | Stapeworms
Nursing Takeaway: Treat the whole family to stop the cycle. Cut fingernails short and wash bed linens in hot water to prevent re-swallowing eggs.

3. Ectoparasiticides

Used for: Lice, Scabies, and Mites on the skin.

Common Drugs: Permethrin, Malathion, Lindane, Benzyl Alcohol.
Topical application ⬇️ Enters parasite nerves ⬇️ Locks sodium channels open ⬇️ Paralysis & Death

Simple Explanation: These are like nerve gas for external bugs. They lock open the sodium channels in the insect's nervous system, causing their nerves to fire uncontrollably until they paralyze and die.

ADR Mnemonic: "S.K.I.N."
• Stinging | Keep out of eyes | Irritation | Numbness
Nursing Takeaway: Time is important! Leave the medication on for exactly as long as prescribed (e.g., 8 hours). Don't wash it off too early.
