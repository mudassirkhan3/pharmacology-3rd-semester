Antiparasitic Master Guide

1. Antiprotozoals

Concept: Chemicals that hunt single-celled organisms. They sabotage the parasite's internal machinery, disrupting vital metabolic functions like DNA replication, causing the parasite to self-destruct.

Common Agents:
  • Metronidazole, Tinidazole
  • Chloroquine, Hydroxychloroquine
  • Atovaquone
Detailed Clinical Uses:
  • Malaria (prophylaxis)
  • Giardiasis & Amoebiasis
  • Trichomoniasis
  • Toxoplasmosis
Drug enters Parasite
↓
Activation into toxic radicals
↓
DNA Strand fragmentation
↓
Parasite Death
ADR Mnemonic (M.E.T.A.L.): Metallic taste, Epigastric distress, Toxic to CNS, Abdominal cramps, Leukopenia.
Pearl: No alcohol! Causes a "Disulfiram-like" reaction (violent vomiting). Warn patients about dark/red urine (harmless).

2. Antihelminthics

Concept: Designed for worm infestations. These target the parasite's nervous system or structural proteins. They effectively starve the worm or paralyze it, so it loses its grip on the intestine and is passed out.

Common Agents:
  • Albendazole, Mebendazole
  • Ivermectin
  • Praziquantel
Detailed Clinical Uses:
  • Pinworms & Roundworms
  • Tapeworms (Taenia)
  • Hookworms
  • Strongyloidiasis
Drug binds to Tubulin/Nerve receptors
↓
Blocking nutrient absorption
↓
Flaccid paralysis
↓
Expulsion via bowel
Uses Mnemonic (W.O.R.M.S.): Whipworms, Oxyuriasis, Roundworms, Migrating larvae, Tapeworms.
Pearl: Whole-family treatment is required. Wash linens in hot water. Cut fingernails short to break the fecal-oral cycle.

3. Ectoparasiticides

Concept: External armor-piercing agents. These drugs absorb into the exoskeleton of surface parasites (lice/mites). They disrupt nerve cell channels, causing unceasing twitching and eventual death.

Common Agents:
  • Permethrin
  • Malathion
  • Spinosad
Detailed Clinical Uses:
  • Head & Body Lice
  • Pubic Lice (Crabs)
  • Scabies mites
Topical absorption
↓
Sodium channel over-stimulation
↓
Continuous nerve firing
↓
Death by paralysis
ADR Mnemonic (S.K.I.N.): Stinging, Keep out of eyes, Irritation, Numbness.
Pearl: Leave on for the *exact* required time. Isolate non-washable items in bags for 14 days (parasites die without a host).
