Antiparasitic Pharmacology Guide

Simplified mechanisms, clinical uses, and high-yield nursing takeaways.

1. Antiprotozoal Agents

Targeting malaria, giardia, and amebiasis.

📂 Mechanisms & Drugs

Metronidazole
MOA: DNA Damage Interrupts DNA synthesis in anaerobic organisms.
Chloroquine
MOA: Heme Accumulation Prevents detox of heme, poisoning the parasite.
Uses: P.A.M. (Protozoal diarrhea, Amebiasis, Malaria)
ADRs: M.E.T.A.L. (Metallic taste, Epigastric distress, Toxic to CNS, Abdominal cramps, Leukopenia)
💡 Clinical Nursing Takeaways:
  • Metronidazole: ABSOLUTELY NO ALCOHOL. It causes a disulfiram-like reaction (violent vomiting, flushing, tachycardia).
  • Chloroquine: Perform baseline and periodic eye exams; it can cause retinal toxicity.

2. Antihelminthic Agents

Treating worms (roundworms, tapeworms, flukes).

📂 Mechanisms & Drugs

Albendazole
MOA: Microtubule Inhibition Starves the worm by stopping glucose intake.
Ivermectin
MOA: Chloride Channels Causes flaccid paralysis of the worm.
Uses: W.O.R.M.S. (Whipworms, Oxyuriasis/Pinworms, Roundworms, Migrating larvae, Stapeworms)
ADRs: A.B.D.O.M. (Abdominal pain, Bone marrow suppression, Diarrhea, Out-of-sorts/malaise, Mazzotti reaction)
💡 Clinical Nursing Takeaways:
  • Whole-House Therapy: If treating pinworms, the entire family must be treated simultaneously to prevent re-infection.
  • Hygiene: Wash all bed sheets and clothes in hot water. Teach patient to cut fingernails short to prevent fecal-oral spread.

3. Ectoparasiticides

Topical treatment for lice (pediculosis) and scabies.

📂 Mechanism & Drug

Permethrin
MOA: Nerve Paralysis Blocks sodium channels, causing paralysis and death to lice/mites.
Uses: L.I.C.E. (Lice, Itch/Scabies, Crabs/Pubic lice, Ectoparasites)
ADRs: S.K.I.N. (Stinging, Keep away from eyes, Irritation, Numbness)
💡 Clinical Nursing Takeaways:
  • Application: Apply topically, leave on for specified time (usually 8-14 hours for scabies), then wash off thoroughly.
  • Contact Isolation: Do not share towels, brushes, or clothing. Place stuffed animals/non-washables in a sealed plastic bag for 14 days (parasites die without a host).

⚠️ Critical Note

Always screen for pregnancy or breastfeeding status before starting anti-parasitic treatment, as some agents cross the placenta or enter breast milk.
