1. Antiprotozoals

Mechanism of Action (MOA)
1. Drug enters parasite cell
↓
2. Reduced to toxic radicals
↓
3. DNA strand fragmentation
↓
4. Parasite replication death

Concept: These agents act as intracellular saboteurs. They enter the single-celled organism, get activated into toxic chemical radicals, and systematically destroy the parasite's DNA, preventing further replication.

Common Drugs:
  • 1. Metronidazole / Tinidazole
  • 2. Chloroquine / Hydroxychloroquine
  • 3. Atovaquone

Clinical Uses:

  • Malaria (treatment & prophylaxis)
  • Giardiasis & Amoebic dysentery
  • Trichomoniasis (STI)
  • Toxoplasmosis
ADRs & Mnemonic: M.E.T.A.L.
(Metallic taste, Epigastric distress, Toxic to CNS, Abdominal cramps, Leukopenia)

2. Antihelminthics

Mechanism of Action (MOA)
1. Bind to Tubulin/Nerves
↓
2. Block nutrient/glucose absorption
↓
3. Flaccid paralysis of worm
↓
4. Expulsion by bowel movement

Concept: These drugs focus on structural starvation and immobilization. By targeting the worm's internal protein network (tubulin) or nerve receptors, they stop the worm from feeding or moving. Once paralyzed and starved, the worm loses its grip and is naturally expelled.

Common Drugs:
  • 1. Albendazole / Mebendazole
  • 2. Ivermectin
  • 3. Praziquantel
  • 4. Pyrantel Pamoate

Clinical Uses:

  • Pinworms & Roundworms
  • Tapeworm infestations
  • Hookworms
  • Strongyloidiasis
Uses & Mnemonic: W.O.R.M.S.
(Whipworms, Oxyuriasis/Pinworms, Roundworms, Migrating larvae, Stapeworms)

3. Ectoparasiticides

Mechanism of Action (MOA)
1. Absorbed into shell
↓
2. Sodium channel lock
↓
3. Continuous nerve firing
↓
4. Paralysis & Death

Concept: These are powerful, surface-acting neurotoxins. They penetrate the hard shell of parasites living on the skin, "jamming" their sodium channels open. This causes uncontrollable nerve firing that leads to total paralysis and death of the pest.

Common Drugs:
  • 1. Permethrin
  • 2. Malathion
  • 3. Spinosad
  • 4. Benzyl Alcohol

Clinical Uses:

  • Head & Body Lice (Pediculosis)
  • Pubic Lice ("Crabs")
  • Scabies Mites
ADRs & Mnemonic: S.K.I.N.
(Stinging, Keep out of eyes, Irritation, Numbness)
