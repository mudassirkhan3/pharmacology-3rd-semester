💊 Antiparasitic Pharmacology

1. Antiprotozoal Agents

Metronidazole
Chloroquine
Primaquine
Pentamidine
Pyrimethamine

⚙️ MOA Detailed Flow

Drug Entry → Intracellular Reduction/Activation → Formation of Toxic Free Radicals → DNA/Heme Disruption → Cell Death
Mnemonic (ADRs): M.E.T.A.L.
• M-etallic taste | E-pigastric distress | T-oxic to CNS | A-bdominal cramps | L-eukopenia
Nursing Pearl: NO ALCOHOL (Disulfiram reaction). Monitor liver/kidney function. Dark urine is a normal, harmless side effect of Metronidazole.

2. Antihelminthic Agents

Albendazole
Mebendazole
Ivermectin
Praziquantel
Pyrantel Pamoate

⚙️ MOA Detailed Flow

Drug Binding → Microtubule Inhibition / Chloride Channel Lock → Glucose Starvation / Muscle Paralysis → Detachment from Intestinal Wall → Expulsion via Peristalsis
Mnemonic (Uses): W.O.R.M.S.
• W-hipworms | O-xyuriasis (Pinworms) | R-oundworms | M-igrating larvae | S-tapeworms
Nursing Pearl: Treat the WHOLE family. Wash all bedding in hot water. Cut fingernails short. Watch for the "Mazzotti Reaction" (fever/rash due to rapid worm death).

3. Ectoparasiticides

Permethrin
Malathion
Lindane (Restricted)
Crotamiton

⚙️ MOA Detailed Flow

Topical Application → Penetration of Exoskeleton → Sodium Channel Blockade → Constant Nerve Firing → Paralysis/Death
Mnemonic (ADRs): S.K.I.N.
• S-tinging | K-eep away from eyes | I-rritation | N-umbness
Nursing Pearl: Leave on for the *exact* prescribed duration (e.g., 8–14 hours for Scabies). Bag non-washable items in sealed plastic for 14 days to starve parasites.
