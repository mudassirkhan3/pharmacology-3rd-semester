Quinolones & Fluoroquinolones

Antibiotics ending in "-floxacin" that stop bacteria from copying their DNA.

📂 Clear Drug Names & Uses

CiprofloxacinCommonly used for severe UTIs and stomach infections.
LevofloxacinRespiratory infection drug ("respiratory fluoroquinolone").
MoxifloxacinAdvanced respiratory drug used for lung infections.
NorfloxacinMainly used for urinary and gut infections.
OfloxacinStandard older fluoroquinolone antibiotic.
⚡ Flow Animation: Mechanism of Action (DNA Replication Arrest)
🧬
Step 1: Unwinding Bacterial DNA begins active replication and supercoiling.
🎯
Step 2: Enzyme Binding Drug targets & binds DNA Gyrase (Topo II) & Topo IV.
✂️
Step 3: Strand Break Prevents DNA religation, causing lethal double-stranded breaks.
💥
Step 4: Cell Death Bacterial cell undergoes rapid, bactericidal death.

💡 Uses with Mnemonic: "F.L.U.O.R.O."

FFluid infections / UTIs
LLung pneumonia infections
UUrogenital STIs
OOsteomyelitis bone infections
RResistant anthrax
OOcular severe conjunctivitis

💡 Adverse Effects with Mnemonic: "T.E.N.D.O.N."

TTendonitis & Achilles tendon rupture IMP FOR EXAM
EECG QT prolongation
NNeurological seizures
DDysglycemia
OOtotoxicity
NNausea & C. diff colitis

🚫 Contraindications IMP FOR EXAM

Avoid in children under 18 and pregnant women due to cartilage and joint damage risks. Black box warning for tendon rupture.

Folic Acid Synthesis Inhibitors

Drugs starting with "Sulf-" that block bacteria from making their own folic acid (vitamin B9).

📂 Clear Drug Names & Uses

SulfamethoxazoleUsually combined with trimethoprim for broad coverage.
SulfisoxazoleQuick-acting oral drug for basic UTIs.
SulfadiazineOften used for parasite infections like toxoplasmosis.
Silver SulfadiazineTopical cream applied to severe burn wounds to prevent infection.
SulfasalazineUsed for inflammatory bowel conditions like ulcerative colitis.
SulfacetamideEye drops used for pink eye / bacterial conjunctivitis.
⚡ Flow Animation: Mechanism of Action (Competitive PABA Blockade)
🧪
Step 1: PABA Mimicry Sulfonamide enters bacterial cell as a structural PABA analog.
🛑
Step 2: Enzyme Competition Competitively blocks Dihydropteroate Synthase active site.
🚫
Step 3: Synthesis Halt Halts bacterial folic acid building blocks.
🦠
Step 4: Growth Stasis Inhibits nucleic acid replication (bacteriostatic stop).

💡 Uses with Mnemonic: "S.U.L.F.A."

SSimple UTIs
UUlcerative colitis
LLung PJP pneumonia prophylaxis
FFacial/body burn wounds
AAcute toxoplasmosis

💡 Adverse Effects with Mnemonic: "S.K.I.N.S."

SSevere SJS/TEN hypersensitivity rashes IMP FOR EXAM
KKidney crystal nephropathy
IIcterus / kernicterus in neonates IMP FOR EXAM
NNausea
SSunlight photosensitivity

🚫 Contraindications

Contraindicated in near-term pregnancy, nursing mothers, and neonates under 2 months due to kernicterus risks.

Folic Acid Antagonists

Drugs that block the enzyme (DHFR) that activates folic acid inside the bacterial cell.

📂 Clear Drug Names & Uses

TrimethoprimOral agent concentrating heavily in urine and prostate fluid.
PyrimethamineMainly used to treat protozoan and parasite infections.
⚡ Flow Animation: Mechanism of Action (DHFR Inhibition)
🔄
Step 1: Intermediate Build Dihydrofolate accumulates inside the bacterial cytoplasm.
🎯
Step 2: Enzyme Target Trimethoprim selectively binds Dihydrofolate Reductase (DHFR).
🔒
Step 3: Reduction Block Prevents conversion into active Tetrahydrofolate cofactor.
🛑
Step 4: Translation Freeze Shuts down thymidine and purine synthesis (bacteriostatic).

💡 Uses with Mnemonic: "U.R.I.N.E."

UUncomplicated acute lower UTIs
RRecurrent cystitis prophylaxis
IInflammatory prostatitis
NNon-sulfur UTI alternative
EEffective uropathogen clearance

💡 Adverse Effects with Mnemonic: "B.L.O.O.D."

BBone marrow suppression (megaloblastic anemia)
LLiver enzyme bumps
OOral rash
OOccasional GI upset
DDecreased renal clearance

🚫 Contraindications

Avoid in patients with documented folate deficiency, baseline megaloblastic anemia, or during the first trimester of pregnancy.

Combination of Synthesis Inhibitor & Antagonist

Combining a sulfonamide and trimethoprim creates a powerful double-action bactericidal punch.

📂 Clear Drug Names & Uses

Co-Trimoxazole / TMP-SMXKnown by brand names like Bactrim or Septra; widely used for UTIs, MRSA, and pneumonia.
⚡ Flow Animation: Mechanism of Action (Sequential Dual-Blockade)
1️⃣
Step 1: First Block Sulfonamide blocks initial Dihydropteroate synthesis.
2️⃣
Step 2: Second Block Trimethoprim blocks downstream Dihydrofolate Reductase.
🔗
Step 3: Sequential Synergy Simultaneous two-step knockout of the bacterial folate pipeline.
💥
Step 4: Bactericidal Kill Synergistic shift converts static drugs into a rapid bactericidal kill.

💡 Uses with Mnemonic: "P.N.E.U.M.O." IMP FOR EXAM

PPJP pneumonia treatment/prophylaxis in HIV
NNocardiosis
EE. coli UTIs
UUpper/lower respiratory infections
MMRSA skin infections
OOsteomyelitis

💡 Adverse Effects with Mnemonic: "R.A.S.H.E.S."

RRash / SJS reactions
AAnemia in G6PD deficiency
SSerum hyperkalemia (potassium retention)
HHepatic necrosis
EElevated creatinine
SStomach upset

🚫 Contraindications

Contraindicated in infants under 2 months, term pregnancy, nursing mothers, and G6PD deficiency.

Urinary Tract Antiseptics

Drugs concentrated by the kidneys to specifically target and clean out bladder and urinary tract infections.

📂 Clear Drug Names & Uses

NitrofurantoinAvailable as Macrodantin or extended-release Macrobid; frontline choice for uncomplicated bladder infections.
MethenamineTurns into formaldehyde in acidic urine to kill bacteria.
FosfomycinTaken as a convenient single-dose powder sachet mixed in water.
⚡ Flow Animation: Mechanism of Action (Urinary Concentration & Activation)
💧
Step 1: Filtration Drug filters rapidly through kidneys, concentrating in bladder urine.
⚙️
Step 2: Enzymatic Conversion Bacterial flavoproteins reduce drug into active intermediates / formaldehyde.
🔨
Step 3: Macromolecule Damage Damages ribosomal proteins, DNA, or denatures microbial structures.
✨
Step 4: Local Eradication Safely sterilizes urine and bladder without heavy systemic toxicity.

💡 Uses with Mnemonic: "C.Y.S.T.I."

CCystitis acute lower UTIs
YYeast/bug suppression in catheters (Methenamine)
SSingle-dose female eradication (Fosfomycin)
TTargeting resistant E. coli
IInfectious flare prevention

💡 Adverse Effects with Mnemonic: "P.U.L.M.O."

PPulmonary toxicity / fibrosis
UUrine brownish-orange discoloration
LLiver hepatitis
MMegalo/hemolytic anemia in G6PD
OOptic neuritis

🚫 Contraindications IMP FOR EXAM

Nitrofurantoin is contraindicated in significant renal impairment (CrCl < 30-60 mL/min) because it fails to concentrate in urine and accumulates toxically in blood.
