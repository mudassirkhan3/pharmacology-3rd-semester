Essential Clinical Ward Protocols & Nursing Measures

  • 1
    Culture & Sensitivity Timing: Always obtain targeted diagnostic cultures (blood, urine, sputum, or wound swabs) prior to administering the first loading dose of antibacterial therapy.
  • 2
    Antimicrobial Stewardship & Compliance: Educate patients thoroughly on completing the entire prescribed therapeutic course to prevent resistant bacterial strain development.
  • 3
    Hypersensitivity & Cross-Reactivity Screening: Perform meticulous allergy reconciliations regarding beta-lactams and monitor patients closely during the initial infusion window.
  • 4
    Therapeutic Drug Monitoring (TDM): Rigorously track serum peak and trough concentrations for narrow therapeutic index agents to optimize bacterial eradication safely.
